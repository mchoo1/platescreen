`dietTags: ["halal"]` at the outlet level is a step beyond the existing sibling operator entries (`kopitiam`/`koufu`/`foodfare` all carry `dietTags: []`). This is justified, not guessed: multiple independent sources (Zabihah halal directory, Yelp listings for the VivoCity and Woodlands Square branches, general Singapore food-court write-ups) consistently describe Banquet as Singapore's first all-halal food court chain (opened at Jurong Point in 1999), where every individual stall is halal-certified — unlike a typical mixed food court where halal status varies stall-by-stall.

- [Singapore Food Insights Database — Health Promotion Board](https://www.hpb.gov.sg/healthy-living/food-and-beverage/sgfoodid/) and [NutriKaki](https://nutrikaki.com/nutrition/) — generic nutrient references used to sanity-check macro ranges for the hawker/food-court dishes below (same secondary sources used for the Kopitiam/Koufu/Foodfare sessions).

No Banquet-specific per-dish nutrition page or PDF was found — consistent with the queue entry's note ("Use HPB nutrient data for stall dishes") and the outcome for the other three operator entries already in the database. One dead-end note: an older "Banquet Now Bankrupt?" (2013) blog post and a mention that the VivoCity/HarbourFront "Banquet Food Hall" location became Kopitiam suggested the chain may have contracted since its original scale — but the Jurong Point, Woodlands Square, and (a differently-numbered) VivoCity/Harbourfront Walk listings all show as active in 2025–2026 sources, so the operator brand is still current and in scope.
