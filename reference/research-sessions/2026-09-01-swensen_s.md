## Research (Phase 2)

No official Swensen's Singapore nutrition/calorie source exists:
- `swensens.com.sg`'s own PDF menu (`wp-content/uploads/2024/06/swensens-menu.pdf`)
  has no calorie/macro data (confirmed via WebSearch snippet quoting its text).
- HPB's Healthier Choice Symbol covers packaged retail products, not restaurant
  menu items — not applicable here.

Dish names and prices sourced from `singaporerestaurantshub.com/swensens-menu-prices/`
(a menu-price aggregator), cross-verified two ways:
1. A WebSearch snippet directly quoting the official PDF menu's own text confirmed
   the Southern Style Chicken 'n' Waffle price ($16.90) and the Golden Classics
   price band ($17.90–$18.90) exactly matched the aggregator's figures.
2. foodpanda outlet listings (Plaza Singapura, Junction 8, Bedok, Compass One)
   independently confirmed the Sourdough 49ers Chicken and Bagus Impossible™ Burger
   item descriptions word-for-word.

A second aggregator, `thefoodprices.com`, was checked and **rejected** as a source:
its "calories" column was suspiciously uniform (every single value a multiple of
50, including implausible ones like "2 kcal" for espresso) and several unrelated
items across different categories shared identical odd price endings (e.g. $19.15,
$17.01, $18.08 recurring across burgers, pasta, and grilled items) — a pattern
consistent with fabricated/templated content rather than a real scrape. No data
from this source was used for names, prices, or macros.

## Diet tags

Swensen's Singapore is MUIS halal-certified — confirmed via multiple independent
per-outlet listings on halalboleh.com (VivoCity, Junction 8, Parkway Parade, and
others), not a single unconfirmed claim. Applied `halal` at Brand level (matching
the existing `mcd`/`kfc`/`burger_king`/`subway` pattern) and to every MenuItem
(matching `mcd`'s own per-item halal tagging, e.g. Big Mac). The Bagus Impossible™
Burger also gets `vegetarian` (plant-based Impossible patty, no meat).
